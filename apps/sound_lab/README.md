# Sound Lab

Windows, Android, Linux. Built on `app_shell`, `audio_kit`, `chip_synth`
and `fx_kit`.

- Sources: analog synth, retro chips (with their hardware quirks), drawn
  waves from Waveform Painter (with morph), or a sample/recording played
  chromatically.
- Up to 8 effects, reorderable, each with on/off and its own knobs.
- Live waveform / spectrum (tap the scope).
- Sounds save to the Asset Locker as shared `sound` presets; render a note
  or a processed sample to WAV (saved to Samples or exported).
- Keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play, Z/X octave.
