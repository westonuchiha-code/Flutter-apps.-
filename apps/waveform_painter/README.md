# Waveform Painter

Windows, Android, Linux. Built on `app_shell`, `audio_kit` and `chip_synth`.

- Draw one cycle of a wave (freehand or line), or start from a shape or a
  picture (brightness of a row, or the outline). Smooth, mirror, normalize,
  invert, reverse, undo.
- Up to 4 waves (A–D) with a Morph slider between them.
- Play on a multi-touch piano (2–3 octaves; keyboard A W S E D F T G Y H U J K,
  Z/X octave). Sound sheet: envelope, glide, filter, sub, drive, vibrato,
  echo, reverb.
- Save waves to the Asset Locker (presets other synths can load); save a
  rendered note as a sample (for Sample Pad); single-cycle WAV export.
