# Sample Pad

Windows, Android, Linux. Built on `app_shell`, `audio_kit` and `beat_kit`.

- 16 pads (multi-touch; keyboard 1234/QWER/ASDF/ZXCV, Space play/stop).
  Sounds from the Asset Locker (WAV, up to 30 s) or the mic; trim, pitch,
  reverse, volume, pan, one-shot/hold/loop.
- Step sequencer (16 or 32 steps), tempo, tap tempo, swing.
- Loop Station: record mic layers 1–8 bars long, starting on the next bar,
  locked to the beat; mute, level, undo, clear. Use headphones; adjust
  "Loop timing" in Settings if loops sound early or late.
- Kits (pads + pattern + tempo) save to the Asset Locker as presets; the mix
  renders to WAV (saved to Samples or exported).
